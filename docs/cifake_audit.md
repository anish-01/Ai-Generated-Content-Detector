## Manual review notes

Reviewed both contact sheets (seed 42) by eye on 2026-11-11.

**All 40 samples opened correctly and every label looks plausible. No image is
clearly mislabelled.**

- **REAL (20/20 plausible):** typical CIFAR-10 photos (dogs, deer, frogs, horses,
  ships, planes, trucks, cars, a bird) with cluttered backgrounds, uneven lighting
  and off-centre subjects.
- **FAKE (20/20 plausible):** look like Stable Diffusion output: centred subjects,
  smooth blurred (bokeh-style) backgrounds, saturated colours and clean
  "studio-like" framing.

Marked for manual review (uncertain, **not** evidence of a wrong label):

| class | split | file | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| FAKE | test | `333 (6).jpg` | greyscale and the subject is hard to make out |
| FAKE | train | `1173.jpg` | plane has an odd, smeared shape (could just be the 32x32 resolution) |
| REAL | test | `0142 (5).jpg` | subject unclear (deer/animal against a cluttered background) |
| REAL | test | `0691 (3).jpg` | subject unclear |

Caveat: at 32x32 nobody can prove by eye that an image is real or AI-generated.
This check only confirms that the folders don't look obviously swapped.

### Things to handle later

- **Duplicates / leakage:** 1,336 groups of byte-identical images, 378 of which
  appear in both train and test. None span REAL and FAKE, so this isn't a label
  conflict, but the train/val/test split work should dedupe by hash to avoid
  leaking test images into training.
- **Shortcut risk:** REAL and FAKE look different in style (cluttered vs. smooth
  backgrounds), so a model may learn "blurry background = fake" rather than
  generator artefacts. The cross-dataset tests against GenImage should catch this.
